# Quality Dimensions

- `trigger_quality`: does the skill trigger at the right time with enough specificity
- `instruction_clarity`: does the body tell the agent exactly how to use the skill
- `resource_design`: are scripts, references, and assets organized appropriately
- `determinism`: are fragile or repetitive operations pushed into scripts
- `validation_strength`: are there smoke inputs and expected outputs
- `update_safety`: does the skill describe boundaries and failure modes clearly
