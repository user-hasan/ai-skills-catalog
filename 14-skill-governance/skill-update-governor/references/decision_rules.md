# Decision Rules

- `approved`: the request is in scope, source-compliant, and low enough risk to proceed
- `needs review`: the request is valid but shared, breaking, or sensitive enough to need a human or higher-level decision
- `needs policy review`: the request is otherwise valid but relies on trustworthy sources outside the current strict allowlist and must stay draft-only until a source-policy decision is recorded
- `rejected`: sources or scope violate policy
- `deferred`: the request is real but not actionable yet
